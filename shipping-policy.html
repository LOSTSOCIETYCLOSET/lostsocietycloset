Shipping Policy

At Lost Society, we appreciate everyone who supports our brand. Our goal is to provide a smooth shopping experience for every customer. Thank you for being part of the society.

1. Shipping & Delivery Times

Orders are expected to arrive within 5–7 business days, depending on where you live.

Please note that delivery times are estimates and may vary depending on your location, shipping carrier, holidays, or unexpected delays.

2. Order Processing

Orders are prepared for shipment as quickly as possible. During high-demand periods, sales, or new product drops, processing times may be longer than usual.

3. Order Tracking

Want to know where your package is? Click the Tracking tab at the top of our website to check your shipment's tracking information and delivery status.

Please allow time for tracking information to update after your package has shipped.

4. Shipping Costs

Shipping costs are calculated at checkout and displayed before you complete your purchase.

5. Incorrect Shipping Addresses

Customers are responsible for entering the correct shipping address at checkout. Please double-check your information before placing your order.

If you notice an error, contact our support team as soon as possible. Address changes cannot be guaranteed once a package has been processed or shipped.

6. Missing or Undelivered Packages

If your package never arrives, please contact our support team at lostsocietysupport@gmail.com for assistance.

If your tracking information shows that your package was delivered but you have not received it, check around your delivery location and contact your shipping carrier if necessary.

7. Stolen Packages

Lost Society is not responsible for packages that are stolen after delivery. We cannot guarantee replacements or refunds for stolen packages.

If you believe your package has been stolen, please contact your shipping carrier or local authorities. You may also contact our support team with any questions.

8. Customer Support

If you experience any issues, questions, or concerns regarding your order, shipping, or delivery, please always contact our support team.

Email: lostsocietysupport@gmail.com

Support Hours: Monday–Sunday, 6:00 PM–10:00 PM

Please allow time for our team to respond to your message.

Thank you for shopping with Lost Society and supporting our brand.
